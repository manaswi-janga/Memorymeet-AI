\# MemoryMeet AI



> Prepare smarter. Remember every conversation. Build stronger client relationships.



MemoryMeet AI is an AI-powered meeting preparation and relationship-memory assistant. It helps teams prepare for client meetings by recalling important information from previous conversations and turning it into a structured, actionable meeting brief.



\## Problem



Important client information is often scattered across meeting notes and previous conversations. Before a new meeting, teams may spend time searching for:



\- Previous requirements and interests

\- Client concerns and priorities

\- Important stakeholders

\- Competitors and pricing discussions

\- Previous commitments and next steps



MemoryMeet AI uses persistent AI memory to bring this context together before the next meeting.
